## What everyone agreed on

1. **Nothing says you can try before paying, or what happens when a video fails.** Every user and the ASO expert named this. It is the top complaint about both competitors, and the listing still has TODOs there.
2. **The twerk/jiggle tiles next to the grandma hug make the app look cheap.** Jess, Marco, Linda and the policy reviewer all raised it.
3. **The listing is polished but looks like a third copy of iMagic/LitAI.** Same icon concept, no trust message, no promo video.

## Fix list, ranked

### Must fix before submitting
1. **Fill the three TODOs with true facts** — free first video / daily credit, credits back on failure, photo retention, support email. Delete any line that isn't true. (Policy: Blocker; all users.)
2. **Feature graphic: side-card labels are cut off** ("AI Hu", "Dance"). (Jess.) *Template fix.*
3. **Mermaid (ss6) and Anime→Real "Real" half (ss7): covered-up versions.** Policy calls the mermaid a Blocker and ss7 High; Marco also flags ss7.
4. **Real-output rule:** before launch, run the input photos through the real app effects and swap any frame that looks different from what users get. Add one real app-UI frame if possible. (Policy.)

### Strongly recommended
5. **Dance screenshot:** keep Phut Hon as the hero; relabel the four tiles neutrally ("Party Dance", "Groove", "Shake It", "Bounce") or drop them; keep the real effect names in-app only. (Policy High; 3 users.)
6. **Replace ss8 AI Art with a trust screenshot** — "Video Failed? / Credits Back." or "Try One / Before You Pay" — whichever is true. (ASO; Jess 3/10, Marco 4/10.)
7. **Brand name "Pixel AI"** reads as a Google Pixel product (Jess; policy flags IP risk). Consider a different brand before launch, while it's cheap to change.
8. **Remove the "French Kiss" chip** from ss1 and the description (policy Medium). Keep it as an in-app effect.
9. **Description rewrite:** open with "AI kiss video and AI hug video maker…", use each key phrase 3–4 times, add "photo to video", "animate old photos", "hug video generator"; Title Case headers instead of ALL CAPS; drop "Pixel 1.0 to 4.0 models"; add video length/quality; add "Tips for old printed photos". (ASO, Linda, Marco.)
10. **ss6 headline:** "Become a / Mermaid." with subline "Plus alien abduction and liquid metal". (ASO.)

### Test after launch (store listing experiments)
- Icon: tighter crop on faces, bigger "AI" on a hot-pink pill; variant with a play badge. (ASO C+ at 48px.)
- Titles: "AI Kiss & Hug Video Maker" (25) vs "AI Kiss, Hug & Dance Video" (26).
- Short descriptions: keyword-led vs trust-led ("Try your first one before you pay", only if true).
- Promo video, 20–30 s, kiss result in the first 2 s — neither competitor has one.
- Separate hug-first custom listing for "ai hug old photo" searches (Linda): softer colours, daughter-and-mum hug, creased print.
